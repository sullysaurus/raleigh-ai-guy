# Phase 3b: Competitive Meetup Landscape

Research snapshot: July 21, 2026. Public organizer pages and event listings were used; member and attendance counts are point-in-time observations, not audited totals.

## Executive read

Raleigh and the Triangle have plenty of AI activity, but it clusters into three formats: technical builder communities, broad AI talks, and one-off small-business workshops or panels. No durable recurring Raleigh series was found with the exact AI for Business combination: **owners/operators only + nontechnical + a real workflow built live + monthly cadence**.

That is a real opening, but not an empty market. A canceled Raleigh Monthly AI Roundtable used almost the same owner-operator promise. RDU SMB drew 37 people to an AI panel. Institutional calendars contain frequent AI-for-business workshops. The advantage has to come from execution: a dependable venue, a disciplined 90-minute format, visible business math, and recurrence.

## Profiles and countermoves

### 1. Raleigh Monthly AI Roundtable — closest direct concept

The planned event promised that operators would document one business job and delegate it to AI. It was free, 90 minutes, no-pitch, hybrid, capped at 50, and explicitly for business operators. That is very close to this Rock. The event was canceled and still needed a location on its listing.

**Countermove:** publish the first three dates together, confirm the room before promotion, and end event #1 by opening registration for event #2. Reliability is part of the product.

Source: [Meetup event listing](https://www.meetup.com/meetup-group-wiwawuhi/events/313943679/)

### 2. RDU SMB Meetup — strongest audience competitor and partner

Its AI in Action event drew 37 attendees to a general SMB gathering with a practitioner panel, food, and roughly 90 minutes of networking around a one-hour program. It already has the precise owner/operator audience, but AI is a topic rather than the community's sole purpose.

**Countermove:** keep the AI for Business promise narrow: one operational problem, one workflow, one result. Ask for a guest announcement or co-hosted follow-up instead of competing for the same list.

Source: [RDU SMB event](https://luma.com/xwltbuvy)

### 3. All Things AI and AI Tinkerers — attention and talent, not format twins

All Things AI is the large broad tent. AI Tinkerers is the credible builder room, with screened technical demos and a wider 245-city network. Both can absorb AI-interested attendees, but neither centers the small-business owner who does not want a technical deep dive.

**Countermove:** make “no code, no deck, show the business result” explicit. Treat these communities as speaker pipelines and offer a translated, owner-facing stage for strong demos.

Sources: [All Things AI](https://www.meetup.com/en-au/rdu-artificial-intelligence-meetup-group/), [AI Tinkerers Raleigh](https://raleigh.aitinkerers.org/)

### 4. One-off workshops — recurring competition for the calendar

SAM IT Solutions advertised a practical agentic-AI session for owners, and the current Raleigh Small Business Event Calendar contains AI-powered consulting, AI-era digital marketing, and AI grant-writing sessions. SCORE also included AI for Business in its May program.

**Countermove:** check the city calendar before locking each date, submit the listing as soon as the venue is confirmed, and position the meetup as the recurring place to see implementation rather than another introductory class.

Sources: [SAM event](https://www.samitsolutions.com/ai-event), [City of Raleigh calendar](https://raleighnc.gov/doing-business/services/small-business-event-calendar), [SCORE Raleigh program](https://www.score.org/nc/raleigh/business-education/national-small-business-week-score-raleigh/)

## Positioning to own

> A monthly working session for Raleigh small-business owners. One real business problem, one AI workflow built live, and the numbers that show whether it is worth doing. No technical background and no sales pitch.

Avoid claiming that there are no competitors. Use the sharper distinction:

- Not a technical builder meetup.
- Not a broad AI panel.
- Not a one-off vendor workshop.
- Not general networking with AI in the title.
- A recurring owner room built around one operational result.

## Competitive operating rules

1. **Venue is strategy.** Never publish until the room and backup are confirmed.
2. **Publish the series.** Showing three dates signals durability that the closest failed concept lacked.
3. **Protect the demo.** One live local workflow should occupy the center of the event; panels are easier for competitors to copy.
4. **Show business math.** Time saved, leads recovered, or revenue protected is the language that separates this from technical groups.
5. **Partner before buying reach.** Prioritize RDU SMB, AI Tinkerers, All Things AI, Bull City AI, SCORE, and the city calendar.
6. **Watch monthly.** Recheck direct Meetup/Luma listings and the Raleigh calendar at T-5 weeks before every event.

Pattern check that validates the pick: Triangle AI meetups run out of coworking spaces and food halls, free or near-free (American Underground and SplatSpace in Durham, Transfer Co. in Raleigh). Raleigh Founded and Loading Dock are the Raleigh-side equivalents.

## Recommendation

**Primary: Raleigh Founded, Warehouse.** Downtown, credible with a business crowd, room for growth to 100, and the win-win pitch is strongest: 25-50 non-technical business owners in their lobby monthly is exactly their membership pipeline. Their site's "bookings Mon-Fri 8am-5pm" line looked like a risk, but the channels research resolved it: **Triangle Startup Collective already meets Monday evenings at 6 PM at a Raleigh Founded location (509 W North St)**, so external evening meetups demonstrably happen there. Confirm which location and the comp terms on the call, and name-drop that precedent.

**Backup: The Loading Dock, Dock 1053.** Free parking, A/V included, transparent pricing. If Raleigh Founded cannot do evenings or wants full rate, Loading Dock caps the downside at roughly $400-600 per event, and the same free-host pitch may zero that out.

Needs a phone call to confirm: Raleigh Founded evening policy + comp deal + projector/mic + parking guidance; Loading Dock evening availability + comp policy; Transfer Co. common-area meetup terms.

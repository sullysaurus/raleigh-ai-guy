# Phase 6: Ops SOP (solo operator edition)

Written so Danny can run every event in about 3 hours of ops work per month, or hand this file to a future assistant unchanged. Dates below are anchored to event #1 (Wed Aug 26); repeat the same offsets for #2 (Sep 23) and #3 (Oct 28).

## T-minus checklist

### T-5 weeks (week of Jul 20)
- [ ] Send venue pitch email (Phase 1 copy). Follow-up bump 4 days later if no reply.
- [ ] Confirm venue in writing: date, 5:00 PM access for setup, capacity, wifi SSID + password, projector or TV input, table for food, parking instructions.
- [ ] Create Luma event with Phase 3 copy and Phase 4 registration questions. Set capacity 10 above room max to absorb no-shows (typical no-show rate 30 to 40 percent for free events).
- [ ] Create the Notion attendee database (Phase 4 schema).
- [ ] Order or make signage: one entry sign ("AI for Business Raleigh -> this way"), one table-top QR sign for check-in. One-time cost, reused monthly.

### T-4 weeks
- [ ] Publish Luma page. Post LinkedIn announcement (Phase 3, post 1).
- [ ] Cross-list on Meetup.com if pursuing that channel this quarter.
- [ ] Submit to Raleigh Chamber / community calendars (Phase 3 channel list).
- [ ] Join target Facebook groups (Phase 3 list). Do NOT post yet; comment helpfully on 2 or 3 threads per group first so the eventual post is from a familiar name.
- [ ] Send 10 personal invite DMs (Phase 3 template).

### T-3 weeks
- [ ] Facebook groups: publish new-member intro post in fresh groups, value post in established ones (Phase 3 calendar and copy).
- [ ] Newsletter section goes out with regular send.
- [ ] Send 10 more personal DMs. Track count on the scoreboard.
- [ ] Confirm guest speaker for THIS event and open conversation with next month's.

### T-2 weeks
- [ ] Facebook groups: question/discussion post variation in remaining groups.
- [ ] Short posts (X or LinkedIn) 1 and 2.
- [ ] Check registration count against milestone (15 by T-1 week for event #1). If short, double personal DMs; they convert best.

### T-1 week
- [ ] LinkedIn 1-week-out post (Phase 3, post 2).
- [ ] Luma blast to registrants: "one week out, here is parking info."
- [ ] Order food for pickup or delivery day-of: 5 to 6 large pizzas (or one deli platter) + 2 cases of water + soft drinks for 25 to 30 people. Budget below.
- [ ] Build or update the 3 slides: title card, structured-networking prompt, CTA slide with QR codes (free review + next event).
- [ ] Dry-run the live demo end to end on the laptop that will present, on a phone hotspot (assume venue wifi fails).

### T-1 day
- [ ] LinkedIn day-before post (Phase 3, post 3). Short posts 4 and 5.
- [ ] Luma reminder blast (automatic, verify it is on).
- [ ] Pack: laptop + charger, HDMI and USB-C adapters, phone hotspot tested, name tags + markers, signage, QR stands, tape, trash bags.
- [ ] Confirm food order and venue access time by text or phone.

### Day of (Wed)
- [ ] 4:45 PM arrive. Signage up, A/V tested with actual laptop, demo tab open, food table set, check-in QR at door.
- [ ] 5:30 doors. Danny at the door greeting and checking in (Phase 4 process).
- [ ] Photos: ask one friendly attendee to take 10 to 15 phone photos during demo and networking (content for next month's promo). Get one full-room shot.
- [ ] Run the Phase 2 run of show. Hard stop 7:00.
- [ ] Before leaving: photograph the check-in count, thank the venue contact in person, confirm next month's date with them.

### T+1 day
- [ ] Morning: score every attendee in Notion (Phase 4 rubric). HOT emails actually go out the same evening as the event; verify all sent.
- [ ] Send NURTURE thanks + recap email and no-show email.
- [ ] Post 1 recap on LinkedIn with the room photo and one takeaway. Tag the speaker and venue.

### T+2 days
- [ ] Log scoreboard metrics (Phase 8).
- [ ] Book discovery/review calls that came in. Update Outcome column.
- [ ] Write down 3 things to fix for next event. Fix them in this SOP.

# Phase 7: Organizer Playbook Call

The original prompt referenced a specific friend's meetup as the model. Adapted for Danny: book one 30-minute call with an established Triangle meetup organizer who has run a monthly business or tech event for 12+ months (candidates surfaced in the Phase 3 research; any organizer of a 100+ member Raleigh-Durham Meetup group works, and organizers almost always say yes to "I am starting a complementary event, can I ask you 30 minutes of questions").

Questions, ordered by importance. Get through the first six no matter what.

1. **Attendance curve**: What were your real attendance numbers for events 1 through 6, and what single thing moved the number most?
2. **What flopped early**: What did you try in the first 3 months that you would tell me to skip entirely?
3. **Venue economics**: How is your venue deal structured? Free in exchange for what, exactly? Has the venue ever pushed back, and what keeps them happy?
4. **The regulars**: What percentage of attendees are repeats by month 6, and what do you deliberately do to create regulars?
5. **Attribution**: How do you track which attendees turn into revenue, and what would you change about how you did it?
6. **Do differently**: If you restarted from zero today, what are the 2 or 3 things you would do differently from day one?
7. **Speaker sourcing**: Where do your speakers actually come from, and how far ahead do you book? What makes you reject one?
8. **Sponsors**: If you take sponsors, how do you keep the event from feeling sold? If you refuse them, why?
9. **No-show rate**: What is your registered-to-attended ratio, and does anything actually improve it?
10. **Channel truth**: Of everything you do to promote, what actually fills seats, and what is wasted effort?
11. **The awkward stuff**: How do you handle pitch-y attendees, MLM recruiters, and people who try to hijack the room?
12. **Cadence check**: Has monthly ever felt too frequent, and how do you protect your own energy running it solo?

Close with: "Who else runs a good room in the Triangle that I should talk to, and can I mention you sent me?"

Offer in return: cross-promotion of their event to the AI for Business list, and a guest demo at their meetup if it ever fits.

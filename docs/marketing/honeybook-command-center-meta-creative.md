## Higgsfield master prompt — 15-second vertical Meta ad

Create a premium 15-second vertical video ad in 9:16 for a custom business dashboard service aimed at owners of event, wedding, photography, creative, and professional-service businesses who already use HoneyBook.

Visual direction: editorial business documentary meets high-end product interface. Deep forest green, warm ivory paper, restrained burnt orange accents, subtle film grain, crisp natural light, confident and sophisticated—not flashy SaaS neon, not generic AI imagery, no purple gradients, no robots, no floating holograms. Use realistic laptop and phone screens with original fictional dashboard UI only; do not reproduce HoneyBook’s interface or use private customer information.

Scene 1, 0–3 seconds: overhead desk shot. A business owner rapidly switches between a HoneyBook-style client-workflow tab, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Search Console, and a messy spreadsheet. The screens feel fragmented. Camera pushes in with controlled urgency. On-screen text: “Five tools. One unanswered question.”

Scene 2, 3–7 seconds: the tabs collapse smoothly into one elegant private command center. Show fictional metrics for new inquiries, booked revenue, ad spend, leads needing replies, proposals viewed, and connection health. On-screen text: “What needs attention today?”

Scene 3, 7–11 seconds: close-up of a clear priority queue. Highlight “Meta lead needs reply,” “Proposal viewed twice,” and “High-intent SEO page.” Then show a simple attribution line from ad spend to inquiry to booking to collected revenue. On-screen text: “Connect HoneyBook to the whole business.”

Scene 4, 11–15 seconds: clean hero frame with the dashboard on a laptop, Raleigh AI Guy visual branding in a tasteful corner, and warm orange action color. On-screen text: “Complimentary Command Center Blueprint” followed by the single CTA: “Map my command center.” End with URL: raleighaiguy.com/honeybook-command-center

Motion: deliberate camera moves, seamless interface transitions, subtle depth, 24fps cinematic motion, readable screens, no frenetic cuts. Keep all typography large enough for mobile. No voiceover baked into the video. Leave clean rhythm and pauses for a separate voice track.

## Production notes

- Generate the video without logos from third-party platforms unless you have permission to use them.
- Add the Raleigh AI Guy logo, exact typography, captions, and final URL during editing so they remain sharp.
- Use hook 1 for the first ad. Test hooks 3 and 6 as the next two opening-text variants while keeping the body, offer, landing page, and CTA unchanged.
- Send traffic to the landing page with Meta UTMs so the page can pass campaign attribution into Calendly.

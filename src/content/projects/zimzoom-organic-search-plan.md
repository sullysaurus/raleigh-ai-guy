## Overview

[ZimZoom Photo Booth](https://zimzoomphotobooth.com) already had something many SEO projects lack: real search visibility. The problem was that most of that authority lived on the homepage while duplicate, underpowered, and unfocused pages competed for the same searches.

I turned three Keywords Everywhere exports into a visual growth plan that connects demand to the exact URLs already ranking. The result is not a generic content calendar. It is a sequence for protecting current visibility, consolidating overlap, strengthening commercial pages, and publishing only where the data shows a useful gap.

## The Diagnosis

The analysis covered 59 seed keywords, 147 unique domain-ranking keywords, and 40 reported top pages with URL-level keyword mappings.

- An estimated 74% of reported organic traffic was concentrated on the homepage
- Three URLs competed for Raleigh searches
- Two 360 photo booth URLs split the topic without owning local intent
- Only five of 40 reported URLs received estimated traffic
- Proven pages already ranked for Backdrop, Glam, and Roamer searches and needed protection—not replacement

## The System

### Map demand to the page that actually ranks

Every priority combines search volume, current position, commercial relevance, and the exact ranking URL. That makes it possible to see whether the right page is winning, the homepage is carrying too much, or multiple pages are competing with each other.

### Fix the site map before adding content

The first moves consolidate Raleigh and 360 URLs, redirect low-value attachment pages, and establish conversion baselines. New articles wait until the site has a cleaner structure and authority can move into focused destinations.

### Protect winners while closing gaps

The plan separates proven pages from genuine opportunities. Existing Backdrop, Glam, and Roamer visibility is expanded carefully. Charlotte, wedding, corporate, private-event, and pricing topics become focused pages because the data shows demand and a weak or misplaced current result.

### Turn priorities into a release sequence

Recommendations are organized into three phases: fix the map, transfer authority, then fill the gaps. Each phase names the URL, ranking signal, action, and intended outcome so the strategy can move into production without becoming a pile of disconnected SEO tasks.

## The 90-Day Roadmap

- Weeks 1–4: consolidate competing URLs, clean up attachment pages, and set conversion baselines
- Weeks 5–8: rebuild Charlotte, strengthen Raleigh and 360 pages, and upgrade the Backdrop gallery
- Weeks 9–12: publish cost, wedding, event, and corporate hubs tied to commercial search intent
- Review visibility and engagement monthly, then use qualified inquiries and booked events as the business scorecard

## How AI Fits

AI can accelerate data organization, pattern finding, draft prioritization, and the production of a clear client-facing report. The evidence remains the control layer: current rankings, exact URLs, real search demand, business relevance, and human review determine what makes the roadmap.

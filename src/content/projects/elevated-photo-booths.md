## Overview

[Elevated Photo Booths](https://elevatedphotobooths.netlify.app/) provides premium photo booth rentals for weddings, corporate events, parties, proms, and quinceañeras across Raleigh–Durham and beyond. I designed and built a complete website that presents the company as an experience partner—not simply an equipment rental.

The site brings four booth options, the event experience, social proof, the veteran-owned business story, and a focused quote flow into one expressive system. It gives prospective clients the confidence to choose a booth while keeping the energy and spontaneity of a great celebration front and center.

## The Challenge

Photo booth businesses often rely on similar lists of features, which makes it difficult for a customer to understand why one experience will feel different from another. Elevated needed a site that could communicate both the quality of the output and the warmth of the service behind it.

The website also had to make four distinct offerings easy to compare, work for several event types, and move visitors from inspiration to a useful inquiry without flattening the personality of the brand.

## The Experience

### Let the guests sell the feeling

The opening uses a full-screen celebration photograph, editorial typography, and the line “The party looks good on you.” The first impression is built around the outcome clients want: their favorite people relaxed, connected, and having a genuinely good time.

### Make every booth easy to understand

The DSLR Studio Booth, 360 Video Booth, iPad Selfie Booth, and All-Day Drop-Off each get a clear visual card, concise description, and best-fit event cues. Visitors can scan the lineup quickly or select a booth to carry that choice directly into the quote form.

### Turn service into part of the product

The three-step experience explains how the team helps choose, customize, deliver, support, and collect each setup. Reviews and the veteran-owned story reinforce that the value is not only in the camera—it is in having the event handled well.

### Keep the inquiry personal

The quote flow asks for the date, event type, booth preference, and a little about the vision. It gives the team useful context for a helpful response while keeping the interaction light and low-pressure for the customer.

### Carry the energy across screens

Responsive layouts preserve the oversized type, immersive imagery, booth comparisons, and clear calls to action on mobile. Navigation and forms remain straightforward even as the art direction stays bold.

## Why It Matters

The best event websites do more than display packages. They help people picture how a celebration will feel and make the next step easy when that feeling clicks. Elevated Photo Booths now has a digital presence that looks as polished as its photos, explains the value behind the service, and turns excitement into a better booking conversation.

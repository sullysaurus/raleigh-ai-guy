## Overview

[Karen Rabins Therapy](https://karenrabinstherapy.com/) is the private practice of Karen Rabins, a Licensed Clinical Social Worker serving adults and seniors in Raleigh, North Carolina. Karen wanted a very simple website, so I designed and built one that feels calm, personal, and easy to understand.

The site gives prospective clients the essential information they need—who Karen helps, her clinical specialties and treatment approaches, what to expect, and how to contact her—without adding complexity or pressure.

## The Goal

Looking for a therapist can already feel overwhelming. The website needed to reduce that burden, not add to it. Visitors should be able to quickly understand Karen's experience, decide whether her practice might be a good fit, and find a clear way to reach out.

The project was intentionally modest in scope. The work was choosing the right information, organizing it carefully, and presenting it with enough warmth and credibility to support a very personal decision.

## The Approach

### Keep the experience calm

A restrained teal palette, generous space, peaceful natural imagery, and approachable typography create a welcoming tone. The design supports Karen's voice without making the practice feel overly clinical or promotional.

### Answer the important questions

The page structure follows the questions a prospective client is likely to have: Who is Karen? Who does she work with? What concerns does she address? How does therapy work? What should I expect? How can I get in touch?

### Make contact easy

Contact remains visible in the primary navigation, while phone and email details are easy to find throughout the experience. The path from learning about the practice to reaching Karen stays direct on desktop and mobile.

### Build trust with useful details

Karen's credentials, more than 30 years of experience, areas of focus, availability, and practice information are presented in plain language. Local search metadata and structured practice information help the site communicate that same clarity to search engines.

## What I Built

- A custom responsive website for Karen's private practice
- Clear pages for her background, clinical specialties, and treatment approaches
- A practical guide to what new clients can expect
- Accessible mobile navigation and readable page layouts
- Direct phone, email, and contact paths
- Local SEO foundations and structured practice data

## My Role

Information architecture, UX and visual design, responsive frontend development, content structure, accessibility, local SEO foundations, and launch.

## Why It Matters

Simple is not the absence of design. For this project, it meant removing distractions and giving each piece of information a clear purpose. The result is a small, approachable website that represents Karen professionally and helps people decide whether they are ready to start a conversation.

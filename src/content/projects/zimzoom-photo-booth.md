## Overview

[ZimZoom Photo Booth](https://zimzoom.netlify.app/) creates photo booth experiences for weddings, parties, university events, and brand activations across North Carolina. I designed and built a complete website that captures the energy of the events while giving customers a clear way to compare experiences, see the work, and check their date.

The site brings six services, real event photography, customization options, statewide locations, reviews, FAQs, and booking into one bold visual system. It presents ZimZoom as more than a booth rental: an experienced creative partner that knows how to make a room come alive.

## The Challenge

ZimZoom has substantial proof—thousands of events, millions of photos, recognizable clients, strong reviews, and a broad service area. The challenge was organizing all of that credibility without losing the playful personality that makes the brand memorable.

The website also needed to distinguish six related experiences, work for both private celebrations and brand events, and give visitors enough information to choose confidently without slowing down the path to availability.

## The Experience

### Open with unmistakable energy

The hero combines real guest photography, oversized typography, animated details, and the promise of “Extraordinary Photo Booth Experiences.” The first screen establishes the brand as colorful, social, and polished while keeping the primary action—checking a date—immediately visible.

### Turn six services into six personalities

Pro, Glam, 360, LITE, Headshots, and Event Photo each receive a distinct card, visual treatment, and plain-language explanation. The system helps visitors understand the difference between experiences while showing that they all belong to the same brand.

### Let scale and real work build trust

Event totals, photo counts, client logos, gallery images, reviews, and media recognition work together as layered proof. Wedding guests, corporate teams, university groups, and event planners can all find evidence that feels relevant to their occasion.

### Make customization tangible

Backdrops, graphics, branded interfaces, galleries, and sharing experiences are presented as visible parts of the product. The site shows customers how ZimZoom can feel specific to their wedding, organization, or campaign instead of relying on a generic customization claim.

### Connect statewide reach to conversion

Dedicated location presentation establishes service across Raleigh, Durham, Charlotte, Asheville, Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and Wilmington. The final booking flow carries the event details into ZimZoom's live request system while phone and gallery paths remain close at hand.

## What I Built

Product strategy, positioning, information architecture, UX and visual design, conversion copy, responsive frontend development, content structure, accessibility, booking flow, and launch.

## Why It Matters

ZimZoom already knew how to make events memorable. The new site gives that experience a digital expression with the same confidence and personality. Visitors can now feel the energy, understand the options, trust the team behind them, and move naturally from inspiration to availability.

## Overview

Prowl.fm is a live music discovery product built around a simple idea: fans should be able to see which rooms are alive right now, and bands should have an easier way to show up where people are already looking.

The product positions itself as a real-time map for live music nearby. Bands go live, fans spot music, and venue pages stay tied to the real places people search for.

## Product Direction

Live music discovery is fragmented. Venue calendars, social posts, search results, and word of mouth all help, but they are rarely connected in a way that feels current.

Prowl.fm turns that into a product surface:

- A live music radar for finding active rooms nearby
- Venue pages connected to real places people search for
- Band and venue flows for keeping live status current
- A map-based discovery experience for nights when people want music now

## Why It Matters

The interesting product challenge is trust. A live music map only works if the signal feels current, useful, and local.

That makes Prowl.fm a good example of the kind of product work I care about: taking an offline behavior, identifying the stale parts of the discovery loop, and building a system that makes the next action easier for both sides of the marketplace.

For fans, that means finding live music without digging through stale listings.

For venues and bands, that means a clearer way to be discovered when the room is active.

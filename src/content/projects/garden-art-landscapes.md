## Overview

Garden & Art Landscapes had more than 20 years of local experience, a deep library of project knowledge, and a new website. The next problem was turning that expertise into a steady local-search system without producing generic SEO filler.

I built a workflow that connects topic research, business knowledge, page production, publishing status, and live measurement in one place. The goal is not simply to publish more. It is to answer the questions Raleigh homeowners already ask and learn from what earns visibility.

## The opportunity

Landscaping searches are highly specific. A homeowner may be looking for help with a retaining wall, drainage, native planting, low-maintenance design, sod, or seasonal work. Each question has a different audience, level of urgency, and path to a project inquiry.

The business needed a way to decide which topics were worth covering, preserve the owner’s real expertise, and see what happened after a page went live.

## What I built

- A topic pipeline organized by customer intent and service relevance
- Research prompts grounded in the company’s actual work and Raleigh service area
- A repeatable page brief and drafting workflow
- Editorial review before anything is published
- Search Console and analytics reporting in the same dashboard
- A simple status system for what is planned, drafted, published, and learning

## Early results

The first cycle produced 18 useful pages with live measurement in place. Early rankings began appearing for several targeted Raleigh landscaping questions, including positions around 4, 14, and 28 while newer pages were still being discovered.

Those numbers are a starting signal, not a victory lap. The value of the system is that the business can now see which topics earn impressions, which pages need improvement, and where the next useful page should come from.

## Why it matters

This is the kind of AI system I like building: the tool does the repetitive research and preparation, the business owner supplies the judgment and expertise, and the results remain visible enough to improve over time.
